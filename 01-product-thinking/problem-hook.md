# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** The company will start losing enterprise accounts to rivals that offer leaner solutions.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Instead of using RouteLogic’s bloated platform for routing, coordinators export data into Excel to manually cross-check carrier rates and routing options, then import the final schedule back into the platform.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve the loss of enterprise accounts to leaner competitors by addressing the heavy administrative burden slowing down frontline coordinators.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For RouteLogic, we will remove workflow friction so frontline coordinators can complete their tasks in seconds because a slow and bloated experience is driving enterprise customers to leaner competitors.
